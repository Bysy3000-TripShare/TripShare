How It Works

A shared trip in three steps.

01. Find your route

Search for a driver travelling between your departure and destination cities.

02. Send a request

Review the trip and fare, then request a passenger seat or space for an eligible small item.

03. Meet and share the journey

In the planned service, payment follows driver acceptance. After the trip, confirm completion and leave a review.

Why TripShare?

TripShare connects drivers and passengers for shared journeys, making travel more affordable and environmentally friendly.
