What Can You Send?

Item Size & Weight

Each item should fit within a medium-sized suitcase or box (up to 65 × 45 × 30 cm and 15 kg).
*Item delivery is subject to driver acceptance and available cargo space.

Eligible Items

Clothing, books, gifts, and small personal belongings.

Restricted Items

Items that are hazardous, perishable, or otherwise prohibited.

Need More Space?

Larger items may be accepted depending on the driver's available cargo space and approval.
